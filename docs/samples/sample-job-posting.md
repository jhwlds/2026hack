# Junior Software Engineer — Brightline Labs (fictional)

Brightline Labs is a fictional 60-person company that builds scheduling software for community clinics.
This document is made up for demonstrations and describes no real company or person.

## The role
Brightline Labs is hiring a junior software engineer for the Platform team, led by Dana Okafor (Engineering Manager).
The stack is TypeScript and Python. New hires ship small features in their first month and pair with a senior engineer,
Priya Nair, during onboarding.

## How Brightline Labs hires
1. A 30-minute recruiter call with Marcus Lee (Talent Partner)
2. A take-home coding assignment of 4 hours. It is unpaid, the evaluation criteria are not published, and candidates
   receive no individual feedback after submitting.
3. A 60-minute technical conversation with the Platform team
4. A final conversation with Dana Okafor

## Benefits
Hybrid work from the Salt Lake City office, 15 days of paid leave, and a learning budget of $1,000 per year.

## The job seeker community discussing this policy
The following fictional individual job seekers talk about Brightline Labs' hiring process in an online community.

- Minjun is a job seeker preparing for a first job. He is building a portfolio, has plenty of free time but little
  experience, and treats every application as important. He cares about whether an assignment helps his portfolio
  and whether he gets feedback.
- Seoyeon is a software developer who is employed full-time and preparing to switch jobs. She can only work on
  assignments on weekday evenings and weekends. She cares about time efficiency and schedule flexibility.
- Jiho is a job seeker going through take-home assignments for several companies at once. Each one asks for hours of
  unpaid work and the deadlines overlap. He cares about the total time required and about compensation for unpaid work.
- Haeun is a job seeker who cares a lot about published evaluation criteria and stated time estimates. She
  reconsiders applying whenever the criteria are unclear.
