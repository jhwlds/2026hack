# Junior Software Engineer — Brightline Labs (fictional)

Brightline Labs is a fictional 60-person company that builds scheduling software for community clinics.
This posting is made up for demonstrations and describes no real company.

## The role
We are hiring a junior software engineer to join the Platform team, led by Dana Okafor (Engineering Manager).
You will work with a TypeScript and Python stack, ship small features in your first month, and pair with a senior
engineer, Priya Nair, during onboarding.

## What we look for
- A computer science degree, a bootcamp, or equivalent self-taught experience
- Comfort reading unfamiliar code and asking good questions
- Clear written communication

## How we hire
1. A 30-minute recruiter call with Marcus Lee (Talent Partner)
2. A take-home coding assignment
3. A 60-minute technical conversation with the Platform team
4. A final conversation with Dana Okafor

## Benefits
Hybrid work from our Salt Lake City office, 15 days of paid leave, and a learning budget of $1,000 per year.
